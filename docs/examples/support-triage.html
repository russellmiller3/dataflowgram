Interactive data flow

Support email, sorted by AI

A customer email arrives. An AI model sorts it, and the urgent ones reach a person at once.

An urgent email

  1. A customer writes in: the app charged them twice.
  2. The sorting program picks up the new email.
  3. The AI model reads the email and returns a topic, an urgency and the mood.
  4. The program files the ticket at the top of the billing queue.
  5. Because it is urgent, the billing lead gets a phone alert.

Source: A made-up example for this project. The names, amounts and emails are invented.
