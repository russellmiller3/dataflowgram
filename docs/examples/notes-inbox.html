Interactive data flow

A notes inbox with AI

Forward an email to one address, and it is filed for you. Ask a question the same way, and you get the answer back.

Files a note

  1. Sam forwards an email.
  2. The hub fetches the email and checks that the sender is on the team.
  3. The hub looks up the sender's company in the lists.
  4. The model reads the text and returns fields: a summary and 1 follow-up.
  5. The hub writes 1 note row and 1 follow-up row.
  6. The hub saves the attachment and sets its tags.
  7. The hub replies with what it filed, in 5 lines.

Asks a question

  1. Sam emails a question: how much did we pay this supplier?
  2. The hub checks that the sender is on the team.
  3. The model works out that this is a question, and what it asks for.
  4. The hub reads the figures from the ledger export.
  5. The reply is the answer in 1 line, then the breakdown.

Source: A made-up example for this project. The names, amounts and emails are invented.
